Handbook / Build your store

FORME / 0.5.6

Put the pieces first.

A useful catalog is the foundation of every product section.

Prepare each product

  1. In Products Add product, enter a clear title, original description, vendor, category, price, and any genuine compare-at price.
  2. Upload at least two or three useful images: a full product view, another angle or worn view, and a material/detail view. Add descriptive alternative text.
  3. Create real options such as Size and Color. Set the price, SKU, weight, inventory, and image for each variant where applicable.
  4. Set the product to Active and include the Online Store sales channel.
  5. Add it to a collection. Choose that collection or product in the theme section that should display it.

Make stock available

Go to Products Inventory, select the correct location, and set a positive available quantity for each sellable variant. The location must fulfill online orders and be included in appropriate shipping profiles.

If you track stock, every variant needs stock at a valid location. If you intentionally do not track stock, turn tracking off for that product. Use “continue selling when out of stock” only when you can fulfill backorders.

Product and inventory CSV files have different jobs.

Import product details on Products. Import the inventory export format on Inventory. Do not put Location, On hand (current), or On hand (new) columns in a product import. Preserve Shopify’s original exported headers, including the Option1/2/3 Name and Value columns when present.

Images and native media

Product pages use the product’s Shopify media gallery, including supported images, videos, external videos, and 3D media. Open an image for the full-screen viewer. Assign variant images so selecting an option can show the relevant piece.

Keep image framing consistent across your catalog. Use Product cards Image ratio for portrait or square cards, and the collection template’s Product image fit to choose contained products or cropped editorial images. A product page shows product media; collection banners are configured separately.

Color swatches

FORME renders Shopify-native option swatch data. Assign the correct product category, connect the color option to its category metafield, and supply color or image swatches in Shopify. An option called “Color” alone does not guarantee a visual swatch. Text options remain available when no native swatch exists.

Recommendations

The product template includes related and complementary recommendation sections. Choose recommendations in Shopify Search & Discovery and confirm the products are published and available. Empty recommendation results do not create dummy cards.

Gift cards and selling plans

Use Shopify’s gift-card product type for gift cards. The product form supports recipient fields and eligible native selling-plan allocations. Subscription or purchase-plan setup comes from Shopify and your compatible app; installing FORME does not create plans. Test the selected plan and its terms through checkout.
