Handbook / Operate your store

FORME / 0.5.6

From selection to checkout.

Native Shopify commerce, enhanced with quick interactions.

Quick view and quick add

Enable Theme settings Shopping Quick actions. Quick view opens the selected product with its current price, image, and available purchase controls. Quick add opens choices for products that need options; straightforward products can be added directly.

Test an available product, an unavailable variant, a multi-option product, a sale product, and a gift card. Check that the chosen options, quantity, plan, and recipient information reach the cart correctly.

Cart drawer or cart page

Set Shopping Cart type to Drawer or Page. The native cart remains available at the cart URL. Update quantities, remove items, add a note, and confirm discounts and totals against Shopify.

When network or server errors interrupt an update, follow the shown recovery message and verify the bag before retrying. The theme does not invent a successful cart state when Shopify has not confirmed it.

Checkout and customer accounts

Checkout, payment authorization, taxes, shipping rates, and account authentication are Shopify-managed. Configure payments, shipping profiles, tax settings, customer accounts, and checkout branding in Shopify. The theme cannot guarantee a payment method or fully restyle Shopify checkout.

The account entry appears when customer accounts are enabled. Use Shopify’s native account configuration; no theme password database is included.

Developer-store tests

Use your development store’s supported test payment configuration and place a complete test order. Verify line items, discounts, taxes, shipping, customer details, and order confirmation. Keep test orders and payment details out of public bug reports. Remove test settings before real commercial operation where required.
