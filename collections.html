FORME / 0.5.6

A collection with presence.

Image-led openings and purposeful product browsing.

Create and assign collections

  1. In Products Collections, create a manual or automated collection. Add products or set its conditions.
  2. Upload its collection image and write its description. Confirm it is available to Online Store.
  3. Choose Default collection, her, or him as its theme template where available.
  4. In the theme editor, open that collection template and preview a real collection using it.

Choose the opening

MastheadUse
Compact browsingA shorter image-led or typographic opening that gets customers to products quickly.
EditorialA more spacious composition for an edited collection.
CampaignA full-width image-led masthead with overlaid identity. Enable the banner and provide the collection image.

In Collection products, set banner visibility, description, kicker, department links, products per page, image fit, vendor visibility, and swatches. Without a collection image, the clean theme uses a typographic opening instead of injecting a demo photo.

Keep separate edits separate

The her and him suffixes are alternate layouts; they do not create, name, or automatically populate collections. Select real department resources in Theme settings, then assign templates to whichever collections you choose.

Merchandise the grid

Set the collection’s default sort in Shopify and use manual ordering for a deliberate opening selection. Start with around 24 products per page. Choose Contain for clean product photography and Cover for consistent editorial crops. Check sale pricing and variant availability against the underlying products.

Product pages use product media

The product page opens with the individual product’s gallery and purchase information. It does not reuse the collection banner as a generic product photo. See Product pages for blocks, gallery behavior, and optional product data.
