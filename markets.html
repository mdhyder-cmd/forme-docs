Handbook / Operate your store

FORME / 0.5.6

A store that travels.

Countries, currencies, and languages are managed by Shopify.

Set up your regions

  1. Open Markets in Shopify. Create or activate the markets you intend to serve.
  2. Check each market’s country coverage, catalog availability, pricing, currency, domain or subfolder, tax display, and shipping eligibility.
  3. Verify that your payment setup supports the desired checkout currencies. Eligibility and available market customizations depend on Shopify, your plan, and your payment provider.
  4. Enable automatic localization/redirection in Shopify where appropriate. Keep country/language selectors available so customers can correct their region.
  5. In Header and Footer, enable localization controls. Enable currency-code display in Theme settings International.

Automatic currency is a platform feature

FORME renders the money and localization Shopify supplies. It does not estimate exchange rates in browser JavaScript or replace checkout currency. Shopify Markets can resolve a customer’s region automatically when configured, while customers can select their country in the theme’s localization controls.

A single configured market may produce only a locale label instead of a selector. A country switch does not create a shipping rate or make unpublished products available.

Languages

Add and publish the languages you support in Shopify, then translate products, collections, pages, menus, theme labels, and section copy using Shopify’s language tools or a compatible app. Included locale files are English; additional languages require real translations. Theme editor text is not translated automatically just because you enable a language.

Test the whole journey

Select a second country and confirm product price, currency code, product availability, cart subtotal, shipping address, shipping rate, and checkout currency. Test on the actual storefront rather than relying only on a market preview. Check regional tax display with your store configuration.
