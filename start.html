FORME / 0.5.6

A considered beginning.

Install FORME, connect your catalog, and build a store that feels like your own.

What is in your download

FileUse
FORME-0.5.6-clean.zipUpload this ZIP directly to Shopify. It contains the native theme folders only.
FORME-0.5.6-github-pages-docs.zipExtract for the offline handbook or publish these documentation files with GitHub Pages.

The clean theme includes the arranged templates, typography, colors, editorial layouts, shopping controls, and motion. Demo photographs, videos, products, inventory, and store-specific resource selections are excluded. Your catalog lives in Shopify.

Your first setup, in order

  1. Upload the clean theme to Online Store Themes Import theme Upload zip file (some admin layouts say Add theme). Keep it unpublished while you work.
  2. Add your products, prices, variants, inventory, and product media. Make them active and available to the Online Store channel.
  3. Create collections and upload collection images. Create the content pages in the page map.
  4. Open the theme editor. Set your logo, department collections, page destinations, and header collection blocks.
  5. Choose the homepage hero media and its links. Then connect the featured collection, shoppable looks, materials, and journal.
  6. Configure shipping, payments, taxes, policies, domains, and optional Markets in Shopify settings.
  7. Complete the launch checklist in your actual store before publishing.
A fresh install starts with your content.

Blank product sections are expected until a real product or collection is selected. Native editor placeholders help you arrange images; they are not demo products.
