Know what travels with it.

Theme ownership, merchant media, and runtime notices.

Original theme code

Use of the original FORME theme files is governed by the commercial agreement supplied with your purchase. No general open-source license or right to resell the theme is granted by publishing this documentation. Keep your purchase and license records.

Your content

The clean theme excludes demo photography, video, and product catalog data. Supply media, brand assets, fonts, and copy you have permission to use. Shopify-provided payment marks, placeholders, font objects, and platform resources remain subject to their own terms.

Bundled animation libraries

LibraryVersionNotice
GSAP and ScrollTrigger3.15.0GSAP Standard No Charge License. Preserve upstream notices. Official terms.
Motion runtime and bundled related packages14.6.0MIT notices are retained in the theme’s assets/motion-license.txt.

The theme includes assets/gsap-license.txt and assets/motion-license.txt. Runtime library rights remain separate from the original theme’s commercial agreement. No animation snippets or templates from third-party effects marketplaces are included.

Fonts and hosting

The theme selects Shopify font-library resources; it does not bundle commercial font binaries. The handbook uses system fonts, local CSS, and local JavaScript, and needs no paid documentation platform.
