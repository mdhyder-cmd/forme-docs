Let the image breathe.

Independent desktop and mobile crops, with optional campaign video.

Recommended image preparation

PlacementStarting pointComposition
Desktop campaignAbout 2400 × 1400 pixelsA wide composition with the subject away from text. Keep the face and garment inside a safe central area.
Mobile campaignAbout 1200 × 1800 pixelsA dedicated portrait crop. Leave room for the overlay copy and controls.
Product cardsAbout 1600 × 2000 pixelsConsistent portrait framing, or a consistent square set if you select square cards.
Collection bannerAbout 2400 pixels widePreview the Campaign, Editorial, and Compact masthead layouts with the actual collection image.
Material / shoppable lookAbout 1600 pixels on the long sideA clean detail image or complete outfit with room for responsive cropping.

These are editorial recommendations, not upload requirements. Shopify generates responsive image sizes. Use clear, compressed originals and avoid embedding text in photography.

Fix a cropped model

  1. In the campaign block, choose separate desktop and mobile images where possible.
  2. Adjust the Desktop focal point horizontally and the Desktop vertical position vertically.
  3. Adjust the Mobile focal point for the portrait viewport.
  4. Preview a wide desktop, a laptop, and a narrow phone. Keep the whole silhouette readable at all three sizes.

Where video is supported

SectionVideo behavior
Full-screen campaign Campaign blockDesktop and mobile Shopify-hosted video, with image posters. Hero video is muted and looped when autoplay is allowed.
Shoppable lookDesktop and mobile video, image poster, optional autoplay and loop; products stay beside or below the media.
Campaign filmDesktop/mobile video and independent posters, optional autoplay and loop, collection link, up to four products. Default is click to play.
Product galleryNative product media from the Shopify product, including supported video and 3D resources.

Add a film

  1. Upload your own licensed campaign clip to Shopify Files or select/upload it through the video picker.
  2. Choose it in the section’s Campaign video setting. Add a separate mobile video if your vertical composition needs it.
  3. Select desktop and mobile posters where those settings are available. Without an independent mobile poster, the theme falls back to available imagery or video preview media.
  4. Choose autoplay and loop where offered. Add a short heading and select the products featured in the film.
  5. Preview on a real phone with sound off, with reduced motion enabled, and on a slower connection.

Respect the viewer

Browser autoplay policies still apply. Autoplay is silent; user-initiated playback can expose native audio controls. Reduced-motion or data-saving preferences keep the poster until the viewer chooses Play. Video pauses when out of view, on hidden slides, in a hidden tab, or when an overlay takes focus.

For lightweight campaign moments, start with a short compressed MP4, around 8–15 seconds, and avoid multiple competing autoplay sections. Provide meaningful text alongside important video content and ensure essential information is also available without audio.
