FORME / 0.5.6

Ready for the real world.

A store acceptance checklist before you publish.

Catalog and editorial content

  • Every visible section uses your real, published content.
  • Hero slides have separate deliberate copy, media, and working links.
  • Products have clear photos, accurate variants/prices, and available inventory.
  • Collection images, sort order, descriptions, and templates are assigned.
  • Lookbook anchors, FAQ answers, care/fit information, journal, and footer links are populated.
  • All demo-specific text and unsupported marketing claims are replaced.

Shopping and integrations

  • Test single-option and multi-option products, unavailable variants, sale prices, quick view, quick add, and the image viewer.
  • Add, update, and remove cart items; test an empty bag, discounts, notes, and cart error recovery.
  • Place a full test order with shipping and taxes; verify the order in Shopify.
  • Test gift cards, selling plans, pickup, and app blocks if your catalog uses them.
  • Apply/reset filters, sort, search, and navigate browser Back.
  • Switch each offered market/language and confirm the resulting checkout.
  • Test contact and newsletter success/error feedback with accounts you control.

Presentation and settings

  • Review a real phone, a narrow desktop window, and a wide desktop.
  • Check hero focal points, overlay-logo contrast, hover menus, tap controls, and sticky UI.
  • Test keyboard navigation, text zoom, reduced motion, and video without audio.
  • Measure performance using populated pages and your enabled apps.
  • Configure real shipping rates, payment providers, taxes, notification emails, domain, and policies.
  • Keep a theme backup and a clear rollback path.

Theme distribution is separate from store launch

This handbook and the clean ZIP are not a Shopify Theme Store approval, legal certification, or a guarantee for every app. A theme sold independently still needs an actual-store acceptance run and clear seller license/support terms. Shopify Theme Store submission has its own requirements and review process.
