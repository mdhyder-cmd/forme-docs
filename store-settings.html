Set the foundations.

The Shopify settings that let the theme do its job.

Configure the store, then the presentation

These are store settings rather than theme switches. Available fields can vary by Shopify plan, region, provider, and admin layout. Use your real business information for a live store and clearly marked test information in a development store.

Shopify areaSet up / verify
Settings GeneralStore name, contact details, business address, time zone, units, and default currency. The store name is used when no header logo is selected and in the footer wordmark.
Settings LocationsOnline fulfillment locations, correct addresses, and product inventory at those locations.
Settings Shipping and deliveryShipping profiles, zones, rates, fulfillment locations, delivery estimates, and optional pickup.
Settings PaymentsAn eligible payment provider and supported methods. Use the supported test configuration for development-store orders.
Settings Taxes and dutiesYour applicable registrations and tax/duty behavior. Verify calculations with the store configuration.
Settings CheckoutCustomer information requirements, checkout settings, and available branding controls.
Settings Customer accountsEnable and configure Shopify customer accounts if you want the header account entry.
Settings NotificationsSender details, customer/order notifications, and contact form receiving address. Send test messages.
Settings PoliciesYour actual privacy, refund, shipping, terms, and contact policies as applicable.
Settings Customer privacyRegion-appropriate consent, privacy banner, marketing/data settings, and integrations.
Markets and LanguagesActive country coverage, catalogs, currencies, published languages, and optional automatic localization.
Settings DomainsThe domain you intend customers to use and its primary-domain behavior.
Settings Custom dataThe optional product fit, care, model, and size-guide definitions described in Product pages.
Content Menus / FilesYour nested navigation, footer menus, licensed images, and campaign clips.
Apps Search & DiscoveryApplicable filters, search merchandising, and related/complementary products.
Online Store PreferencesAvailable SEO/social sharing settings and storefront password protection.
Theme editor App embedsEnable only the compatible app features you actually use.

A strong demonstration catalog

For a substantial fashion demonstration, prepare around 50 original catalog entries with two or three coherent images each, real variant options, prices, and test inventory. Create a small number of well-edited collections and use them consistently in navigation and the homepage. No sample catalog is bundled in the clean theme.

Complete the connection

Store configuration alone does not select content in theme sections. Follow the homepage, navigation, page, and footer guides next. Preview published products and pages within the actual theme, and finish with the launch checklist.
