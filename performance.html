Handbook / Operate your store

FORME / 0.5.6

Keep it effortless.

Media, motion, accessibility, and sensible storefront testing.

Keep the page light

  • Upload appropriately sized, compressed images instead of many oversized originals.
  • Use a short, compressed campaign film and a useful poster. Avoid filling every section with autoplay video.
  • Keep your first product selection focused. Add only the app embeds you use.
  • Recheck the storefront after installing an app or adding a large tracking script.
  • Measure populated mobile and desktop pages. A clean empty install does not represent your real store’s performance.

Motion preferences

Motion can be disabled in Theme settings Shopping Enable motion. The storefront also responds to reduced-motion preferences. Test drawers, the campaign carousel, product image viewer, navigation, and video in both modes.

Animation libraries are bundled as local theme assets. No external animation CDN needs to be configured by the merchant. Keeping these scripts intact preserves their paired behavior and license notices.

Accessibility checks for your content

  • Write useful image alternative text. Leave decorative imagery appropriately decorative.
  • Check text and controls against every chosen color scheme and hero photo.
  • Use headings in a meaningful order. Avoid large text that is only visually a heading.
  • Navigate menus, filters, quick view, cart, and image viewer by keyboard; confirm focus returns to the opener.
  • Check zoomed text and a narrow phone. Do not communicate options through color alone.
  • Caption or transcribe meaningful video speech and keep important information available without sound.

Measure and review

Use Shopify’s storefront performance reporting and browser accessibility/performance tools on the actual populated theme. Local code checks are useful evidence, but do not certify legal compliance, platform integration, real-device behavior, or any promised score.
